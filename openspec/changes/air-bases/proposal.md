# Proposal: air-bases

## Why

The owner (2026-10-24): "We'll need some hanger bays or air base for the player to land and get their
mech fixed... Air bases allow the player to fix their mechs, buy new mechs and get into different
mechs", and "completing missions let you buy more mechs". A career (career) and jobs (mission-jobs)
need somewhere to come home to and something to spend the pay on.

## What changes

- **Five air bases** spread over the island on flat ground by the highways, one of them near the
  start (survey J5). Each has:
  - a landing apron with lit pads;
  - a row of hangar bays sized for 24 m mechs;
  - a repair bay with gantries;
  - a control tower;
  - a ready room with the mail terminal and the mech catalogue.

  All of it is walked and flown in first person; nothing cuts to a menu.
- **Landing** on a pad inside the base's fence counts as arriving: the base becomes your current base
  and the career is saved.
- **Repairs:**
  - You walk the mech into the repair bay and stop. The gantries swing in with a burst of sparks
    and steam, and the repair is done at once (survey J11: instant).
  - It is charged part by part in a fixed order (torso first, then legs, arms, head).
  - Each part costs its share of the mech's repair price through the career's ledger. When the money
    runs out, the repair stops with the rest still damaged.
  - Lost arms and legs are refitted.
- **Buying mechs is what the pay is for:**
  - The catalogue terminal in the ready room lists the roster (Foreman, Ironhorse, Harrow, Roustabout,
    Driller, Mule, Picket, Sapper) with price, mass, speed, armour and guns.
  - Buying one charges the career, and the mech is rolled into a free bay behind closed doors.
  - Job pay is the money that buys them. Every mech is for sale from the start; money is the only
    limit (survey J14: "all mechs are unlcoked, you just dont have money to buy them").
- **Switching mechs:** your other mechs stand in their bays. You walk to one and board it the way you
  board today. The one you left stays parked in its bay with its damage kept.
