# Proposal: mission-jobs

## Why

The owner (2026-10-24): "Also need a mission system. The player will need to open emails and accept
jobs. Fly to a location and get in a fight." Today the game runs one mission, the city defence, from
the start. A career needs a stream of work you choose.

## What changes

- **Mail:** an inbox of messages from clients. Some are job offers: who, what, where, the enemy
  expected, the pay and the time limit. You read it in the cockpit on an MFD page (MAIL), pressing
  it like the other pages (free look and click), or press M to bring the mail screen into focus; at
  an air base it is on the ready room terminal; and Esc in the mech opens the menu, whose buttons
  switch to the other menus, mail among them (owner, 2026-10-25, survey J1: "press M to focus on the
  mail screen, ESC inside the mech will bring up the menu with buttons caan switch to other menus").
- **Accepting a job** marks its site on the nav page and the screens, starts its clock, and declines
  the other offers that clash with it.
- **The site:** when you arrive (within the job's radius), the fight starts: the job's forces arrive
  the way invasion-forces brings them (drops, aircraft, mechs, crabs).
- **The result:** win and the pay goes through the career's ledger; fail (time out, the thing you
  were to protect lost, or your mech destroyed) and you are paid nothing. A destroyed mech ejects
  you: you land on foot and must get to the nearest base yourself, where the mech is recovered with
  a full repair bill (survey J3: "you have to get to the nearest base on foot. whjenever your mech is
  destroyed you eject out of yuor mech"). Either way a message reports
  it, and new offers arrive.
- **Kinds of job, in data:** defend a city (today's defence, one wave or three), intercept a raid
  near a city, and destroy a target (a crab nest or a convoy). Jobs are sited in and near cities, and
  you get there on the jets and skates as they are (survey J2: "jets and skis recharge... Jobs are
  usualy started near or in a city"). New kinds are data plus, at
  most, one rule each in the job registry.
