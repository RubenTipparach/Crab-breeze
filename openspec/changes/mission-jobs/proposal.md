# Proposal: mission-jobs

## Why

The owner (2026-10-24): "Also need a mission system. The player will need to open emails and accept
jobs. Fly to a location and get in a fight." Today the game runs one mission, the city defence, from
the start. A career needs a stream of work you choose.

## What changes

- **Mail:** an inbox of messages from clients. Some are job offers: who, what, where, the enemy
  expected, the pay and the time limit. You read it in the cockpit on an MFD page (MAIL), pressing
  it like the other pages (free look and click), and at the air base on the ready room terminal.
- **Accepting a job** marks its site on the nav page and the screens, starts its clock, and declines
  the other offers that clash with it.
- **The site:** when you arrive (within the job's radius), the fight starts: the job's forces arrive
  the way invasion-forces brings them (drops, aircraft, mechs, crabs).
- **The result:** win and the pay goes through the career's ledger; fail (time out, the thing you
  were to protect lost, or your mech destroyed) and you are paid nothing. Either way a message reports
  it, and new offers arrive.
- **Kinds of job, in data:** defend a city (today's defence, one wave or three), intercept a raid at a
  site in open country, and destroy a target (a crab nest or a convoy). New kinds are data plus, at
  most, one rule each in the job registry.
