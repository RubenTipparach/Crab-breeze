## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Clouds wet the glass
Flying through a cloud SHALL put drops on the glass, using the same drop effect as surfacing from water, rising with the cloud's density and clearing after the eye leaves it.

#### Scenario: Into and out of a cloud
- **WHEN** the eye spends two seconds inside a thick cloud and then leaves it
- **THEN** the glass is wet through, and it is dry again after the drying time
