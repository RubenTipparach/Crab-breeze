## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Mechs are refitted in hangar bays
A mech stopped in a hangar bay SHALL let the pilot fit any weapon the career owns to a station that takes its kind, and the mech SHALL fire the fitted weapon from then on.

#### Scenario: A new gun fitted
- **WHEN** the pilot fits a bought 40 mm cannon to the right arm in a bay
- **THEN** the right arm fires the 40 mm cannon and the WEAPONS page names it
