# Proposal: mech-custom

## Why

The owner, 2026-10-25: "spend money to build, repair or customize your mech". Repair is free in any hangar bay
(`air-bases`); nothing else about the mech can be changed.

## What changes

- **Refit in a hangar bay.** With the mech stopped in a bay, the bay's terminal (and the MECH MFD) offers its
  weapon stations: each arm's gun and the missile rack can be swapped for another the career has bought.
- **Armour.** Each part's armour can be raised a grade for money, up to a limit per mech; heavier armour slows the
  mech a little (its mass is in the simulation).
- **Paint.** A few liveries per mech (the palettes the mech generator already has), free.
- **Bought parts are kept** in the career (`Career.parts`) and fitted or removed freely in a bay.
