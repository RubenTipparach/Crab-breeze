# Proposal: civilians

## Why

The owner, 2026-10-25: "I need a living breathing world where you can walk up and greet people". The cities are
empty of people.

## What changes

- **People on the pavements, in the parks and at the air bases**, walking set routes along the street pieces,
  standing at crossings, sitting on benches; more in the city centres than at the edges.
- **Greeting.** On foot, walk up to someone and press Use: they stop, turn to you and answer with a line that fits
  where they are (a base mechanic, a shopkeeper, a passer-by), from a table in data. Some tell of a job.
- **They react to danger.** Gunfire, explosions and walking mechs nearby send them running from the noise, into
  buildings or away down the street.
- **Built from the humanoid table** (`blender-humanoid-characters`): one rig, one animation library, LODs; crowds far
  off are drawn as instanced impostors.
