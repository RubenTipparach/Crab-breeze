# Proposal: lods

## Why

Owner, 2026-10-24, on the crab enemies: "Probably need lod of everything if we're gonna fight them". A fight puts many
24 m machines in view at once: the detailed mechs run 13,000 to 24,000 triangles each (`assets/models/mechs.json`), and
the crabs, once detailed, will be the same or more. Twelve enemies at full detail is a quarter of a million triangles
before the city, most of them spent on machines a few hundred pixels tall. CLAUDE.md section 10 already asks that every
hero asset ship LODs with a budget each and that the build refuse one over budget; nothing does yet.

## What changes

- **Four levels for every combatant model**: the detailed mechs, the crab mechs, the alien crabs (Skitter, Snapper,
  Big Boy) and the block-outs while they stand in for them. LOD0 is the model as built; LOD1 to LOD3 are generated by the
  same Blender build, per part, so they ride the same bones and the rig, the gait and the damage need nothing new.
- **Budgets in the manifest**, per model and level; the build refuses a level over its budget and `cb-tools validate`
  checks the shipped glbs against the manifest.
- **Switching by distance with a crossfade**: Bevy's `VisibilityRange` on each part's LOD meshes, distances scaled by the
  model's height so a 6 m Skitter and a 30 m Big Boy each change level at the same size on screen.
- **Small parts drop out early**: decals, bolts, hoses and lenses smaller than a set size are not in LOD1 and beyond.
- **The pilot's own mech stays at LOD0** (it is drawn only from outside, while boarding).

## Out of scope

Buildings and city blocks (a later change, see open question L4), the cockpit interiors (seen only from inside),
impostor billboards, GPU instancing of many same-model enemies (`gpu-instancing`).
