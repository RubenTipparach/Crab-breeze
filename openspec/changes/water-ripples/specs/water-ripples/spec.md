## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Splashes make waves
An explosion, a shell, a footfall or a mech landing in water within the live patch SHALL raise a ring of waves that spreads at about 8 m/s and dies away.

#### Scenario: A shell in a lake
- **WHEN** a 6 MJ shell explodes on a lake 50 m from the mech
- **THEN** a ring of waves reaches the mech about 6 s later, and the surface is calm again within 20 s
