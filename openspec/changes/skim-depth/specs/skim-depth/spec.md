## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Skates skim only deep water
A mech's skates SHALL ride the water only where it is deeper than half the mech's height; in shallower water the mech SHALL wade or walk, skates or not.

#### Scenario: Shallow and deep
- **WHEN** a mech with its skates on drives into water 0.4 of its height deep, and another into water 0.6 of its height deep
- **THEN** the first wades on the bed and the second rises to the surface and skims
