## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Rock formations by biome
Sea stacks and arches SHALL stand off the cliff coast, hoodoos and spires in the canyons and boulders on the range and beaches, placed from the seed per tile so a tile grown twice is the same.

#### Scenario: The same tile twice
- **WHEN** a tile's rock formations are grown, let go and grown again
- **THEN** the same pieces stand in the same places
