## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Every house has a drive to a road
Every house the world builds SHALL have a paved or gravel drive from its garage, carport or parking place to the edge of a suburb street or a highway, no steeper than the drive grade limit and crossing no other lot.

#### Scenario: A ranch house in a suburb
- **WHEN** a ranch house is built in a suburb
- **THEN** a drive runs from its carport slab to the street in front of its row, with the curb cut where they meet

### Requirement: Suburb streets join the road network
Every suburb street SHALL be joined, through other streets, to a city's streets or to a highway; a street that cannot be joined SHALL not be built, nor the houses on it.

#### Scenario: Walking out of a suburb
- **WHEN** the pilot on foot follows the streets from any house in a suburb
- **THEN** they reach a city street or a highway without leaving the paving

### Requirement: Businesses have a car park on the road
Every business in a town or at a roadside stop SHALL stand behind a striped car park that opens onto the highway through at least one curb cut.

#### Scenario: A diner on a main street
- **WHEN** a diner is built on a town's main street
- **THEN** a row of striped stalls and an aisle lie between its front and the highway, joined to the road by a curb cut
