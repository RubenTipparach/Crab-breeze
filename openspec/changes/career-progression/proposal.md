# Proposal: career-progression

## Why

The owner, 2026-10-25: "do missions and get money. Then spend money to build, repair or customize your mech. Unlock
new mechs so you can take on riskier endevaors." Jobs today are one flat list. Nothing grows with the player.

## What changes

- **Risk tiers.** Every job has a tier, 1 to 4: a patrol or a small raid (1) up to an assault on a crab nest with a
  Tyrant (4). The pay rises with the tier, roughly doubling a tier.
- **A tier opens with the mech.** The jobs board offers a tier when the pilot owns a mech rated for it (each mech's
  data gets `tier`): the Foreman is tier 1 and 2, a bought heavy mech opens 3, the best opens 4. The mail says when a
  new tier opens.
- **Standing.** Each finished job adds to the pilot's standing with the base that offered it; a failed or abandoned
  job takes some away. Standing raises that base's pay a little and is shown on the mail page.
