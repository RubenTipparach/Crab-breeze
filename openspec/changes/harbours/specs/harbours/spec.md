## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Harbours and ships on the coast
The island SHALL have harbours on its coast with ships moving on sea lanes between them, and shipping jobs SHALL be offered at the harbours.

#### Scenario: A freighter on its lane
- **WHEN** the pilot looks out to sea from a harbour
- **THEN** ships are seen moving along their lanes

#### Scenario: An escort job
- **WHEN** the pilot accepts an escort job at the harbour office
- **THEN** a freighter leaves the quay and raiders' gunboats come for it along its lane
