# Proposal: harbours

## Why

The owner, 2026-10-25: "even ships and mariners". The island has a coast and the sea, and nothing on them.

## What changes

- **Harbours** on the coast by the coastal cities: quays, cranes, warehouses and a harbour office with a job board.
- **Ships at sea**: freighters, tankers and fishing boats on sea lanes between the harbours and off the island, and
  a few at anchor. Their crews (mariners) work the decks and the quays (`civilians`).
- **Shipping jobs** offered at the harbour office and in the mail: escort a freighter along a lane against raiders,
  sink the raiders' gunboats, clear a blockade. A mech that skims the water (`water-skimming`) or flies works the sea.
- **Ships are hit like vehicles** and sink when destroyed.
