The Stratostacean air fortress

A 420 m crab-shell carrier hanging 600 m over the city on eight armoured lift thrusters. It launches fighters off its bow catapults, drops crab mechs on parachutes from its belly, and guards its deck with crab mechs of its own, while its turrets track your mech and fire. Shoot it down: click to fire your mech's cannon, autocannon or missiles at any part. Each part has hit points and armour. The lift thrusters are plated on their outboard side, so get under it. Destroy three on one side and it opens the thrusters built into its hull; their port holes only take hits from directly above or below. Knock out enough of those and it rolls over, falls and crashes. Try Fight to do it from your cockpit.

Rule
Each side carries half the weight (294 MN). An outer thruster carries a quarter at most, so a side down to one cannot hold: it opens its three hull thrusters, a sixth each, and holds again. It falls when a side is short even with them.

Taking it down

  1. Climb on the jets: 600 m takes about 11 s of your 20 s burn. Stay out of the downwash under each thruster.
  2. Your rounds: the 120 mm cannon (260 mm, 90 damage) gets through everything; the 35 mm autocannon (90 mm, 12 a round) only the light parts and the weak points; missiles (150 mm, 45) the cowls.
  3. Land on the deck, a shoulder or a pylon walkway to recharge (8 s). The deck guard comes for you.
  4. Get under it: each lift thruster's outboard side is a 300 mm plate. From below, its nozzle is 40 mm and takes double damage.
  5. Three thrusters on one side and it sinks, then opens six thrusters built into its hull. Their port holes only take hits from within 30 degrees of straight up or down: hover under it or above it.
  6. Knock out two hull thrusters on the weak side and it rolls toward them and falls. Jump clear before the deck tilts past 20 degrees.
  7. Stop its two drive thrusters first if you want it to fall where it is, and not on the city.

Scale

Every crab and the Foreman are the real block-outs, sized by their manifests. The fortress is primitives: it gets its Blender model once this shape is chosen.
