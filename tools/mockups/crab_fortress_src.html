The Hermit air fortress

A 420 m crab-shell carrier hanging 600 m over the city on eight lift thrusters. It launches fighters off its bow, drops crab mechs from its belly on parachutes, and guards its deck with crab mechs of its own. To bring it down you fly up, land on it, and destroy its thrusters: a side left with one cannot hold its half, and the fortress rolls over and falls. Click a thruster, or use the lift board, to try it.

Rule
Each side carries half the weight (294 MN); one thruster carries a quarter at most. A side down to one thruster cannot hold: it falls.

Taking it down

  1. Climb on the jets: 600 m takes about 11 s of your 20 s burn. Stay out of the downwash under each thruster.
  2. Land on the deck, a shoulder or a pylon walkway to recharge (8 s). The deck guard comes for you.
  3. Walk out along a pylon and shoot down into the thruster's intake grille: 40 mm and double damage, where the 140 mm cowl stops your autocannon.
  4. Three thrusters on one side and it rolls toward them and sinks. Jump clear before the deck tilts past 20 degrees.
  5. Stop its two drive thrusters first if you want it to fall where it is, and not on the city.

Scale

Every crab and the Foreman are the real block-outs, sized by their manifests. The fortress is primitives: it gets its Blender model once this shape is chosen.
