Crab Breeze seaport

A 1980s port at true size beside the 24 m Foreman and a 1.8 m pilot. Two breakwaters shelter the basin, with a lit entrance and a buoyed channel. Inside are a container terminal with ship-to-shore gantries, a general cargo pier, a ferry terminal, a tanker jetty with its tank farm, a dry dock, a fishing harbour and a coast guard quay with a slipway a mech can wade down. The ships are the game's own models. Switch to Today to see the harbour as the game draws it now. Nothing here is in the game yet.

A port, not a pier

Today a harbour is one pier on piles with three cranes and three sheds. A real port of the 1980s puts each trade at its own berth: boxes at a straight quay under gantries, break-bulk at a finger pier under luffing cranes, oil at a jetty well away from everything else, the ferry stern-in at a linkspan, the trawlers in their own inner basin. Breakwaters make the calm water all of it needs, and a buoyed channel brings ships in through a lit entrance.

Every building breaks

As at the airport: each building, gantry and tank is made of pieces, and the small-buildings kit's support rule decides what stands after a hit. Take the legs out from under a gantry and its boom comes down into the water.
