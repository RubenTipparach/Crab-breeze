Three airliners, rigged, lit and breakable

The propliner (after the DC-3), the four-jet airliner (after the Boeing 720) and the wide-body (after the L-1011 TriStar), the very files the game will load, at true size beside a 1.8 m pilot and the 24 m Foreman. Raise and lower the gear, spin the props and fans, work the controls, switch to night for the navigation lights, beacons and strobes, cut the fuselage open or walk the aisle to see the cabins, and pull the damage zones apart or click a part to shoot it off and watch the debris fly.

Damage zones

Each aircraft is cut at its airframe's joints into zones: its own meshes, its own hit capsules (measured off the mesh), a parent in a part tree and a share of the aircraft's health. A cut is capped in scorched torn metal; a broken fuselage shows its wall and the cabin. Click one to shoot it off.

Lights

Red on the left tip, green on the right, white on the tail, steady. Red beacons top and bottom flash once a second. White strobes on the tips double-flash every 1.2 s. Landing lights in the wing roots. Switch to Night to see them.

Built by python3 tools/mockups/gen_airliners.py from tools/mockups/airliners_src.html. Blueprints: docs/reference/airliner-blueprints/.
