Your rule: "all buildings are destructible". Each building is made of pieces of at most 10 x 6 x 10 m, and the small-buildings kit's support rule decides what stands after a hit: a piece stands while it reaches the ground through what is under it, or through at most three pieces beside it. Shoot the tower's shaft and the cab comes down; a roof over a small hole hangs on from the pieces beside it, and comes down once more than three in a row have lost what holds them.
