What changes

Today a base is one concrete slab, three mech hangars, three pads, a box tower, and a runway joined by one short taxiway. Proposed, after your four references: a parallel taxiway the runway's length with holding positions, a fully marked runway with approach lights and PAPI, a glazed tower cab, and a terminal with boarding bridges and aircraft on its stands. It also adds a fire station, fuel farm, car park, helicopter stand, windsock and fence. The mech hangars and pads stay where they are.
