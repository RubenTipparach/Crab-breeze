Stance: three blows and it falls

Owner, 2026-10-29: "when hit enough, the stance of enemy mech should break, (usually 3 times) and they fall over". This replaces "every blow knocks it down" in the close-combat design. Not in the game yet: knockdown was still to build.

  • What you see. The first blows stagger the enemy: it rocks back and keeps fighting. The blow that breaks its stance drives it back at 8 m/s. It stumbles a step with its knees buckling, its torso whipping back and its arms flung up. Then it goes over backwards, twisting toward the side the blow came from, with one leg kicking up. It lands about 4 m back from where it stood, bounces twice, skids and throws up dust. It lies there, then gets up through a kneel where it fell. The bar on the viewer fills with each blow.
  • Classes (your answer): a light mech breaks in 2 blows, a mid one in 3 and a heavy one in 4. Light: Picket, Ironhorse. Mid: Harrow, Roustabout, Driller. Heavy: Foreman, Sapper, Mule. Picking a mech in the lab sets its class.
  • Blows. A jab, hook, uppercut or kick counts 1. The overhead smash and the shoulder charge count 1.5. Gun hits never count (your answer).
  • It mends. If 3 s pass without a blow, the stance mends at half a blow per second, so the blows have to come close together.
  • No stun lock. While it is falling, down or getting up, blows add nothing; it stands with its stance whole. Down, it does not walk, turn or fire.
  • Your own mech falls by the same rule (your answer). You stay in the seat, the cabin goes over with you and the screens show the sky.
  • Timing. Stumble and fall 1.0 s, down 1.6 s, getting up 1.3 s: 3.9 s in all.

Decided

S1: 2, 3 or 4 blows by class. S2: guns never wear the stance. S3: your mech can be knocked down too.
