Stance: three blows and it falls

Owner, 2026-10-29: "when hit enough, the stance of enemy mech should break, (usually 3 times) and they fall over". This replaces "every blow knocks it down" in the close-combat design. Not in the game yet: knockdown was still to build.

  • What you see. The first two blows stagger the enemy: it rocks back and keeps fighting. The third breaks its stance. It tips over backwards about its heels, hits the ground and bounces once, lies there, then gets up through a kneel. The bar on the viewer fills a third with each blow.
  • Blows. A jab, hook, uppercut or kick counts 1. The overhead smash and the shoulder charge count 1.5, so two heavy blows break it. A gun's shots do not count (open question S2).
  • It mends. If 3 s pass without a blow, the stance mends at half a blow per second, so the three blows have to come close together.
  • No stun lock. While it is falling, down or getting up, blows add nothing; it stands with its stance whole. Down, it does not walk, turn or fire.
  • Timing. Falling 0.6 s, down 1.4 s, getting up 1.2 s: 3.2 s in all, as before.
