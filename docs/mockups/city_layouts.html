Today

The real rules of cb_core::world::layout: one flat square, a straight grid of 70-110 m blocks with a 36 m arterial every fourth street, every block cut into equal 18-36 m lots, one box on each, taller toward the middle (up to 60 storeys), 6 % of blocks parks, 8 % of lots plazas, a ring of house rows round the edge, straight highways out.

Future

Grown like a real city, in the 1980s industrial setting: a river and an old town of radial lanes on its bend, a tower downtown on its own grid, mid-rise perimeter blocks round courtyards, rowhouse streets, an apartment estate, a port and factories with tanks, stacks and cranes, a rail line and station, a stadium, a central park, a ring road and curving suburbs. Each district on its own grid angle and block size.
