Night window light: glowing glass or a lit room?

A 10-storey glass office tower across a 16 m street, at 22:00. Every number here comes from the bake in openspec/changes/night-voxel-light: the street and the buildings are cut into 0.5 m voxels, light spreads from each source along the shortest path through the air, so it turns corners and stops at walls, and is written into each vertex. Street lamps are the same in all three.
