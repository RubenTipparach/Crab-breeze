Pointing

In free look the mouse turns the head, so the crosshair is the pointer: the screen it falls on is outlined and is the one a click presses. Here the mouse stands in for the head. Nothing ever turns the view for you.

Every screen, every page

Any of the six screens opens the same menu: the five readouts, MAP, MAIL, SHOP and MENU (the pause menu Escape opens). Each screen keeps its own page. BACK, or Escape, closes the menu or app and gives the screen back the page it showed before it was opened; LIST on a shop sheet steps back to the shop.

Buying

  • Every mech is for sale from the start; money is the only limit.
  • The price goes through the career's ledger. Short of money, nothing changes.
  • Away from a base, it waits in the nearest base with a free bay.

The numbers

Heights and masses are read from each mech's game data (data/mechs/<id>.ron); prices are the proposal's (survey J15). The wireframe is drawn here from box proportions; the game turns the mech's own model.
