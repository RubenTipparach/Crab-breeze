Main Street: houses, shops, and shooting them

Seventeen 1980s building types for the island's towns, roadsides, beaches, jungle and desert, built from one kit of parts: walls with real openings, framed and sashed windows with sills and glass you see through, doors and shop fronts, five roof families, porches, interiors with furniture, neon signs, and cars. Walk the street at the pilot's eye, stand at a mech's eye, light it at dusk, or cut it away to see the floor plans. Textures are canvas stand-ins with normal maps for the Material Maker sets.

Building the street

Orbit: drag to turn, right-drag to pan, wheel to zoom. Walk and Mech eye: drag to look, W A S D to move, Shift to hurry. Click (without dragging) to fire the chosen weapon where you point: near pieces blow out, walls break into chunks, anything left hanging creaks and falls, and a building that loses half its ground-floor walls pancakes. Rubble settles into piles. Pick a card to go to that building. The orange block at the end of the street is a 24 m mech.
