Crab Breeze Sounds

The first 36 sounds for the mech, its weapons, what they hit and the cockpit, voiced electronic and futuristic. Every cue the sound and weapon plans name has one: footfalls, servos, the hatch and its steam, jets, every gun from the autocannon to the plasma cannon, lock tones, explosions and alarms.
