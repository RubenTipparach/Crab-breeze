Civilians: the stand-in bodies STAND-IN

The people in the build are box figures made in code, not the humanoid pipeline's bodies: the pipeline's packs (MPFB2, the MakeHuman CC0 assets, the Universal Animation Library) are not on this machine. They are drawn at true human size, each scaled to their own height (1.55-1.95 m), in eight clothing palettes, in the four poses the game uses: standing, the two strides of a walk, and sitting on a park bench (they also lie flat when struck). Beside them: the pilot's 1.8 m rail, a park bench as the game draws it, and the mech's 24 m height. Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, arrow keys and + / - from the keyboard.

Geometry: docs/mockups/civilians_standins.js, written from the game's own body_mesh (crates/cb-app/src/civilians.rs) by its test the_review_page_draws_these_stand_ins, which fails while the file is stale.
In the game, past 60 m a person is a three-box figure (legs, torso, head), and past 250 m a flat card their height, all in one merged mesh.
