The musical world

Rules every cue follows
Home

D natural minor. Every big moment arrives by the lift: B flat, C, then D major. The F sharp of that last chord is the score's signature note, the minor world breaking open when the Breeze wins.

The stride

The mech's gait as a rhythm: 3 + 3 + 2 eighths, stomp, stomp, step-step. The Breeze's melody walks on it; the bass and the kick drum play it.

The crabs

The opposite: chromatic grinding (D against E flat), the tritone (D against A flat), and sixteenths in 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 2 + 2, many legs moving sideways.
