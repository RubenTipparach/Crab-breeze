Crab Breeze Music

Moody, edgy rebel cyberpunk on hard 90s drums. Rain on the city, a snarling riff, a hook built the way classical themes are (one idea, repeated, developed, one high point), big-beat, 909 and rave breaks layered and squashed to sit level with the music. In D minor with the flat two; nothing resolves to a triumphant major chord.

Key D minor, flat twoEdge E flat against DGait 3 + 3 + 2Instruments LMMS synths, 808/909Loudness -16 LUFS

Fourth round, after your notes: “beats are too soft to be edm..we need really strong percussion like edm from the 90s” and “the melody is just garbage”. The drums are layered (909 kit plus a synth kick and clap), compressed and driven, now level with the music instead of 11 dB under it. The hook is rewritten as a sentence from one rhythmic idea. Checked by numbers only; your ears decide.

The mood

An edgy rebel, not a hero. Minor and modal, half-time weight, rain on the city. Nothing cheery, nothing heroic, no victory parade.

The edge

The flat two: E flat against D. The hook leans on it, the spine moves to an E flat chord, the crabs grind on it.

The crabs

Chromatic grinding, the tritone (D against A flat), and sixteenths in 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 2 + 2, many legs moving sideways.
