Crab Breeze military airfield

One of the island's four military bases, at the civil airport's detail and at true size beside the 24 m Foreman and a 1.8 m pilot. A 2.4 km runway and its parallel taxiway, hardened shelters for the fighters and an alert pair at the runway's end, a cargo apron and hangar, a helicopter line, the mech hangars, the tower, the fire station, a fuel farm, munitions bunkers behind earth berms, the living blocks, a radar and two missile sites inside the fence. The fighter, the cargo plane and both helicopters are the models built for the game. Traffic shows how they move. Nothing here is in the game yet.

What moves

A fighter pair leaves the alert apron, taxis to the holding point, lines up, rolls and climbs out with its gear coming up; a gunship lifts off its pad, circles the base and settles again; a cargo plane lowers its ramp and raises it. In the game every aircraft here does this on its own timetable (openspec/changes/airfield-traffic), and the base scrambles its alert pair and a gunship at a mech that attacks it.
