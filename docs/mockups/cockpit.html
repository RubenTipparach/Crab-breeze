Boarding the Breeze

The mech sits down, unlatches, and opens its chest in stages: the armoured visor lifts on its rams with the front screen inside it, the side screens swing out as doors, the flaps fold back, the lower plate drops into a step and the stair unfolds, steam bursting from every seam and cold mist rolling out over the sill. Board it to climb up, sit, and watch it seal and the screens come alive. Inside, the cockpit is sealed: you see out through a wall of screens, as in the references you sent.

The sequence

    What you are looking at

    • The pod: a sealed armoured cockpit at the front of the chest. Everything that moves is a named node the game animates: visor, rams, latches, side doors, flaps, lower plate, three stair sections, the front console, the two grips.
    • The screen wall: one big front screen (it lifts with the visor), an angled screen each side (they are the doors), two behind your shoulders, three monitors hanging from the overhead. They show what the mech's cameras see, with the HUD drawn on the front.
    • Dash and consoles: three MFDs, caution and warning panel, standby gauges, the emergency panel under red guards, the throttle grip in its quadrant with the jet gate, the flight grip on the right, master arm, cyan edge lights.
    • Inside the walls: live circuit boards behind inspection windows with their 880 V warnings, breakers, reactor coolant lines.
