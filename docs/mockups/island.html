The island, at true scale

The 41 km island as the design draws it, from seed 42: a snow range through the middle reaching through the clouds, the desert and its canyons in the rain shadow to the east, jungle and swamps on the wet west, beaches, sea cliffs with stacks and arches, waterfalls where the rivers drop, and an archipelago off the south-west. Ground colours are stand-ins for the twenty Material Maker layers, picked the way the shader will pick them: the two strongest biomes, each by slope. Fly through a cloud to see the rain on the glass.

    What is stand-in

    The ground is a 512 by 512 grid at 80 m, so canyon ledges and cliffs are coarser than the game's quadtree will draw them (to 1 m near the mech). Ground layers are procedural colours, not the Material Maker maps. Trees are placed every 70 m across the whole island; the game grows its dense 30-45 m jungle only near the bodies. Rocks are block-outs of the kit. Clouds follow cloud-puffs: lumpy blobs lit by the sun, wrapped, darker at the base, with a silver lining toward the sun.
