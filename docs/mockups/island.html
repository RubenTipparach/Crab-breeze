The island, at true scale

The 41 km island as the design draws it, from seed 42: a snow range through the middle reaching through the clouds, the desert and its canyons in the rain shadow to the east, jungle and swamps on the wet west, cities by the game's rules joined by highways over the passes, beaches, sea cliffs with stacks and arches, waterfalls where the rivers drop, and an archipelago off the south-west. The weather comes from the west: storm cells with rain shafts over the jungle and the sea, a cloud deck to the north-west, cloud banked on the range, caps on the peaks, fair cumulus thinning over the desert, cirrus high above. Fly through a cloud or under a storm to see the rain on the glass.

    What is stand-in

    The ground is a 512 by 512 grid at 80 m, ten times coarser than the game's 8 m ground cells, so canyon ledges and cliffs are blunter here; each waterfall's face is one steep cell. Even at 8 m a heightmap stretches a cliff into long triangles; terrain-features proposes meshing cliffs from a density field. Ground layers are procedural colours, not the Material Maker maps. Trees are placed every 70 m across the whole island; the game grows its dense 30-45 m jungle only near the bodies. Rocks are block-outs of the kit. Cities follow the game's layout rules, but buildings are plain boxes with a window grid. Clouds are the cloud-weather proposal: one billboard per puff, lit as a sphere, sorted back to front; the weather does not change over time.
