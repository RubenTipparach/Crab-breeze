Crab Breeze · mech design review · third pass, the cockpit in the chest

Pick a chassis and its cockpit

Three new chassis in Mining Mike's boss style, each with its cockpit inside the chest and a different way of opening it. Warden opens like a clamshell over a seated screen-wall cockpit; Lancer opens in petals over a forward-leaning jockey cockpit; Atlas lowers its whole front like a drawbridge over a standing harness cockpit. Press Open to watch the mech kneel, vent and open, or Board it to climb in through the pilot's eyes. All at true scale beside a 1.8 m pilot and a four-storey block.
