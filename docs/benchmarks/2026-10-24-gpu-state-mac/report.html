Crab Breeze · building-research · Apple M4 Max, windowed 120 Hz

GPU Building Updates

City combat (--bench defence), interleaved in one sitting, 2 runs each, windowed at 1920 x 1080 on the M4 Max's 120 Hz display, nothing else running. Not the RTX 3070.

The owner's targets are 120 fps (8.3 ms) at the 95th percentile and never below 60 fps (16.7 ms) at the 99th.

Run 2026-10-24 21:05Build 10201d8 + uncommitted changes GPU Apple M4 Max (Metal)View in the window, presented through the GPU

Findings

  1. Frames are as good as before, and the redraw work is gone. p99 26.55 against 27.00 ms, p95 23.73 against 21.81 ms (inside gpu-shared's 5.67 ms spread), worst 50.6 against 43.7 ms. Slow redraw_buildings frames 12-16 a run against 90-97; what is left is each building's one-time build on its first hit.
  2. A first version with materials per building was three times slower and got worse as the fight went on (p50 16.7 ms in the first minute, 60-80 ms ten minutes in; 20.6 fps). Every damaged building added about 13 materials, so its draws could not batch with any other building's, in every pass. One buffer for all buildings and one material per look fixed it: the frame time stays flat at 16.7 ms for the whole fight.

How this was measured

  • Apple M4 Max at 60 Hz pacing for most of these frames; the RTX 3070 shows how much headroom moved.
  • Draw count is next (building-texture-array): about 10 meshes per damaged building today.

Each run starts the game fresh, plays the scripted take in its window, drawn and presented through the GPU as a player sees it (CLAUDE.md section 7: no offscreen perf rendering), and throws away its first 10 s. A run that crashed, timed out or never finished its take is marked invalid and left out. Traces are binned to 0.1 s: the faint line is each bin's worst frame, the solid line its median.
