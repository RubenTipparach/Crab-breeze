Free-Roam Frame Report

The scripted pilot took jobs across the island overnight while every frame was logged. Fights and fast skating were dropping well under 60 fps; the cause was the CPU working out the ground's height from scratch hundreds of times a step. A height cache fixed most of it.

Still to do

  1. Skating fast. The last condition over 5 % under 60 fps. The frame is CPU-bound with 2 000 to 3 000 meshes drawn and the GPU at about 1 ms: the per-mesh cost of buildings (gpu-instancing) and redraw_buildings (up to 25 ms).
  2. The mech trapped at speed. On a hilltop near (-6049, -7060) the mech stopped dead at 88 m/s, twice, unable to move any way: probably inside a building's cells. A collision bug for its own change.
  3. The six-job loop. The bot drives straight and cannot cross the ridge to the farthest jobs; it needs a road route. One fight also could not be finished in ten minutes.
