Demolition Benchmark (Mac)

Two takes on the owner's Mac (Apple M4 Max, Metal), release builds, run with tools/perf/perf_suite.py, windowed at 1920 x 1080 on the 120 Hz built-in display, nothing else running. This is not the RTX 3070 target.

The owner's targets are 120 fps (8.3 ms) at the 95th percentile and never below 60 fps (16.7 ms) at the 99th.

Run 2026-10-24 16:40Build c85d1de (demolition), 67f4be5 (city combat) GPU Apple M4 Max (Metal)View in the window, presented through the GPU

Findings

  1. 60 fps: yes on average, not on every frame. City combat averaged 66 fps and the demolition 59.9 fps. The median frame is 16.2-16.7 ms (60 fps). The slowest 5 % of frames take about 18 ms (55 fps), the slowest 1 % 21-27 ms (37-48 fps), and there are freezes: one a run of about 80 ms while shelling (once 773 ms), and 21 over 50 ms across the two combat runs. The 120 fps target is missed: only 1 % of demolition frames and 29 % of combat frames are under 8.3 ms.
  2. The demolition misses 120 fps almost every frame. Only 1 % of its frames made the 8.3 ms slot. p50 16.65 ms, p95 18.65 ms, p99 20.90 ms (spread 0.12 ms over 3 runs), peak memory 1222 MB. Over the p95 budget (8.3 ms); the p99 budget (16.7 ms) is missed by 4 ms.
  3. The fall itself is steady: p95 18.48 ms, p99 19.53 ms, worst frame 23.3 ms over 780 frames. The structure step and chunk spawning never logged a slow frame.
  4. Shelling a building freezes the game once: one frame a run of 78-86 ms, once 773 ms. No watched system was slow at that moment; a first-use shader compile or the debris bodies are the suspects. Not proven.
  5. City combat sits on the 120 fps line. 29 % of frames made 8.3 ms, rising from about 20 % in the first minutes to 45-50 % later. p50 16.34 ms, p95 18.07 ms, p99 26.75 ms (spread 0.17 ms), 21 freezes over 50 ms in the two runs, worst 280 ms, peak memory 1459 MB.
  6. redraw_buildings is the cost in combat. Every hit rebuilds that building's mesh on the main thread: 1899 slow frames in run 0 (median 8.6 ms, p95 21.4, worst 33.9 ms) and 1324 in run 1 (median 10.8, p95 25.6, worst 49.1 ms). That alone fills the 8.3 ms budget.
  7. dress_near_player is slow while the city loads (about 100 slow frames a run, worst 29 ms), rarely after.
  8. The building falls differently every run: 176, 144 and 208 of 216 cells standing, same seed. The collapse depends on frame timing, against the determinism rule (CLAUDE.md section 6.3).
  9. The scripted pilot loses in the city. Run 0: the 600 s clock ran out with 9 of 16 enemies left; run 1: the pilot's mech was destroyed. The bot's wall avoidance and unsticking are a pending desktop PR; city 1 took 6-8 % damage.

How this was measured

  • Apple M4 Max, not the RTX 3070.
  • A comparison with shadows, grass and water effects switched off was spoiled by another session's cargo test taking every CPU core.
  • Videos come from separate runs and carry no numbers.

Each run starts the game fresh, plays the scripted take in its window, drawn and presented through the GPU as a player sees it (CLAUDE.md section 7: no offscreen perf rendering), and throws away its first 10 s. A run that crashed, timed out or never finished its take is marked invalid and left out. Traces are binned to 0.1 s: the faint line is each bin's worst frame, the solid line its median. Reproduce with:

python3 tools/perf/perf_suite.py --scenarios demolition --repeats 3 --windowed --out output/perf/demolition-mac
