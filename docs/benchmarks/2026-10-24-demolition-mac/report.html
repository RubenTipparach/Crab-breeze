Demolition benchmark on the Mac

The pilot, on foot, shells every column on one face of a 8-20 storey building, watches it fall and settle, then walks up to the rubble. Three windowed runs at 1920 x 1080 on the Apple M4 Max, nothing else running.

This Mac can't answer the 8.3 ms question today. It held every run to 60 fps: main's own world take, 2.46 ms a frame earlier today, measured 16.67 ms right after these runs. Smoothness and freezes are real; whether the buildings fit the 120 fps budget needs the RTX 3070.

Median over 3 windowed runs, first 10 s thrown away. "Over" is against the budgets, and on a machine capped at 60 fps everything is over the 8.3 ms line by construction. Offscreen runs agree: p50 16.64, p95 18.86, p99 21.83 ms. Peak memory 1.2 GB.

What was found

  1. The fall itself is smooth. 780 frames of collapse with the worst at 23 ms. The structure simulation (step_structures) and the chunk spawning never logged a slow frame.
  2. The freezes are when the shelling starts. One frame a run of 78-86 ms, once 773 ms. No watched system was slow at that moment, so it is outside them: a first-use shader compile or the rubble's physics bodies are the suspects. Not proven yet.
  3. dress_near_player is the slow building system. 164 slow frames over 3 runs (median 7.3 ms, worst 29 ms), 130 of them while the city loads, 34 during the take (worst 15.4 ms). redraw_buildings was slow once a run (5-8 ms).
  4. The building falls differently every run. Same seed, same script: 176, 144 and 208 of 216 cells left standing. The collapse depends on frame timing, which breaks the determinism rule and makes build-to-build comparison of the fall unreliable.

Decision

recommended

Keep the buildings, fix three things

Spread the dressing over more frames, find and kill the shelling freeze, make the collapse step-deterministic. Then measure on the RTX 3070 for the 8.3 ms verdict.

Measure on the 3070 first

Run perf_suite.py --scenarios demolition --repeats 3 --windowed on the desktop before any fix, to see the real headroom.

Cut detail

Shrink the 250 m dressing radius or the building count. Not supported by these numbers yet: nothing here shows the buildings' draw cost.

Limits

  • Apple M4 Max, not the RTX 3070 target, and capped at 60 fps for the sitting (the display was black when checked at 17:10; a 60 Hz external 4K is attached).
  • A shadows, grass and water-effects switch comparison was spoiled by another session's compile taking every core. Its clean early runs showed no change.
