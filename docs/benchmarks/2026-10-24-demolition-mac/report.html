What was found

  1. redraw_buildings is what combat costs. Every hit rebuilds that building's whole mesh on the main thread: 1899 slow frames in run 0 (median 8.6 ms, worst 34 ms) and 1324 in run 1 (median 10.8 ms, worst 49 ms). That alone fills the 8.3 ms budget.
  2. The fall itself is steady. 780 frames of collapse with the worst at 23 ms. The structure simulation and the chunk spawning never logged a slow frame.
  3. The freezes are when the shelling starts. One frame a run of 78-86 ms, once 773 ms. No watched system was slow at that moment, so it is outside them: a first-use shader compile or the rubble's physics bodies are the suspects. Not proven yet.
  4. dress_near_player is the slow building system. 164 slow frames over 3 runs (median 7.3 ms, worst 29 ms), 130 of them while the city loads, 34 during the take (worst 15.4 ms). redraw_buildings was slow once a run (5-8 ms).
  5. The bot loses in the city. The clock ran out with 9 of 16 enemies left in one run; the pilot's mech was destroyed in the other. In the video it spends about 1.5 minutes with its nose in a broken building; wall avoidance is a pending desktop PR.
  6. The building falls differently every run. Same seed, same script: 176, 144 and 208 of 216 cells left standing. The collapse depends on frame timing, which breaks the determinism rule and makes build-to-build comparison of the fall unreliable.

Decision

recommended

Keep the buildings, fix the redraw

Rebuild a hit building's mesh off the main thread (the craters already do), or only its damaged band. Then the shelling freeze and a deterministic collapse, then measure on the RTX 3070.

Measure on the 3070 first

Run perf_suite.py --scenarios demolition,defence --repeats 3 on the desktop before any fix, to see the real headroom.

Cut detail

Shrink the 250 m dressing radius or the building count. Not supported by these numbers yet: nothing here shows the buildings' draw cost.

Limits

  • Apple M4 Max, not the RTX 3070 target.
  • A shadows, grass and water-effects switch comparison was spoiled by another session's compile taking every core. Its clean early runs showed no change.
  • Videos (separate runs, no numbers taken from them): demolition and city combat on Drive.
