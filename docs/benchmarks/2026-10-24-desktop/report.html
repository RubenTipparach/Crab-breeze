Performance suite

RTX 3070 Benchmark

The world, fight and defence takes on the owner's desktop, played in the game's window and presented through the GPU as a player sees it (CLAUDE.md section 7: no offscreen perf rendering). Two variants, two runs each, interleaved: the current build, and the same build with no trees drawn (CB_NO_TREES=1).

The owner's targets are 120 fps (8.3 ms) at the 95th percentile and never below 60 fps (16.7 ms) at the 99th.

Run 2026-10-24 16:52Build 5e93817 + uncommitted changes GPU NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3070 (Vulkan)View in the window, presented through the GPU

Findings

  1. p50 is inside the target on every take: 6.2-7.9 ms (125-160 fps).
  2. p95 misses 8.3 ms on every take, by 0.2-2.3 ms: 9.0 ms on the world take, 10.6 ms on the fight, 10.1 ms on the defence. The trees are not what holds it there: with no trees p95 is 9.0, 10.2 and 9.4 ms.
  3. The trees are where the spikes come from in combat: on the defence take p99 drops from 17.0 to 11.1 ms with no trees, the worst frame from 67 to 23 ms, frames over 50 ms from 1 to 0, and missed refreshes from 2.1 % to 0.7 %. They also cost about 180 MB of memory.
  4. The fight take misses the most refreshes (7.8-9.7 %) with or without trees: the scripted pilot spins its view wildly there (a known bot fault, being fixed), so that take's numbers include a view that sweeps the whole city several times a second.
  5. The GPU is nearly idle: about 2 ms of GPU work a frame with trees, 1.1 ms without. The frame time is CPU work.

How this was measured

Each run starts the game fresh, plays the scripted take in its window, drawn and presented through the GPU as a player sees it (CLAUDE.md section 7: no offscreen perf rendering), and throws away its first 10 s. A run that crashed, timed out or never finished its take is marked invalid and left out. Traces are binned to 0.1 s: the faint line is each bin's worst frame, the solid line its median. Reproduce with:

python3 tools/perf/perf_suite.py --exe target/release/crab-breeze.exe --scenarios world,fight,defence --repeats 2 --variant current --variant "no-trees||CB_NO_TREES=1" --out output/perf/2026-10-24-desktop-windowed
