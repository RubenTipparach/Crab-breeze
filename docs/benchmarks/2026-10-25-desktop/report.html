Performance suite

RTX 3070 Benchmark

The night of 2026-10-25 on the owner's desktop: the world, fight and defence takes, played in the game's window and presented through the GPU as a player sees it (CLAUDE.md section 7). Two changes were measured against the build before them (building dressing off the main thread, burst sizes in steps), and live shadows to 700 m against 500 m. Each pair of variants ran interleaved, two runs each, in one sitting.

The owner's targets are 120 fps (8.3 ms) at the 95th percentile and never below 60 fps (16.7 ms) at the 99th.

Run 2026-10-25 05:35Build d2aa6ed GPU NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3070 (Vulkan)View in the window, presented through the GPU

Findings

  1. With vsync on, what the player gets is 60 fps with drops. The 60 Hz display holds p50 at 15.7 ms, and 9-11 % of refreshes are missed on the world and fight takes (p95 31 ms, a frame that misses waits for the next refresh). output/perf/20261025-baseline.
  2. With vsync off, the frame costs 8-9 ms on the world take, 12-13 ms in the fight and 14 ms in the defence. p95 is 11-13 ms, 15-19 ms and 26 ms. All three are over the 8.3 ms p95 target; the 2026-10-24 report had p50 6.2-7.9 ms on the same takes.
  3. Shadows and buildings cost about 4 ms of CPU each on the fight take (p50 12.6 ms; 8.2 ms with no shadows, 8.7 ms with no buildings). The GPU stays at 1-7 ms: the cost is the CPU work done for every mesh in every view (the main view and three shadow cascades), and each building is its own mesh. This is the next thing to change.
  4. Particles are where the fight's missed refreshes come from: 5.9 % with them, 0.8 % without, p95 19.8 against 14.2 ms. Building new effects mid-fight is not it (every size is now built behind the loading screen and the spikes stay); it is the live effects.
  5. The building hitch is gone: dressing a nearby building took 12-15 ms of the main thread, 45 ms at worst, one building a frame while moving. Meshed off the main thread now, no frame spends over 4 ms on it (232 such frames before on the world take), and freezes over 50 ms fell from 15-17 to 8-9. p50 and p95 did not move: those frames were about 1 % of the take.
  6. Live shadows to 700 m cost the same as to 500 m, inside the spread on both takes.
  7. One frame of 16.4 s in a fight run of the unchanged build, at an ordinary moment (no explosion, no load, nothing in the log). Not explained; not seen again in five other fight runs.

How this was measured

  • Two runs a variant: a difference under the spread is not a result, and most of tonight's are.
  • The fight take is not repeatable enough to judge a change of 1-2 ms; a fixed fight is needed.
  • The defence take has one valid run: the second variant failed to launch (the suite's working directory), to be fixed.

Each run starts the game fresh, plays the scripted take in its window, drawn and presented through the GPU as a player sees it (CLAUDE.md section 7: no offscreen perf rendering), and throws away its first 10 s. A run that crashed, timed out or never finished its take is marked invalid and left out. Traces are binned to 0.1 s: the faint line is each bin's worst frame, the solid line its median. Reproduce with:

python3 tools/perf/perf_suite.py --scenarios fight,world --repeats 2 --variant "before|target/perf-before/crab-breeze.exe|CB_UNCAPPED=1|C:/Users/santi/repos/Crab-breeze" --variant "after|target/release/crab-breeze.exe|CB_UNCAPPED=1|C:/Users/santi/repos/Crab-breeze-close-combat" --variant "after-500m|target/release/crab-breeze.exe|CB_UNCAPPED=1|C:/Users/santi/repos/Crab-breeze/output/perf/root500" --out output/perf/20261025-round2
