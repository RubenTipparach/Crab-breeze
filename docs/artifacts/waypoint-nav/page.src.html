Waypoint Nav

The pause menu's MAP page, on the real seed 42 island (41 km across, from cb-tools mapdata), and the marker it puts in the pilot's view.

Click the map to put down a waypoint (up to 9, in order) · Right click one to take it away · Wheel zooms round the pointer · Drag pans · drag the pilot's view to look round.

ROUTE

In the cockpit

The active waypoint (the first not reached) as the screens show it: a diamond with its number and distance, a tick on the compass, an arrow at the edge when it is off screen. Within 150 m it is taken off with a chime and the next one shows. The mission's waypoint, in gold, goes through the same marker.
