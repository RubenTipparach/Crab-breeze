The island's sites

The island as the game now generates it (seed 42), 40.96 km across: two civil airports placed first, two seaports on different coasts, and four military bases where there were five, every site where the game itself puts it. Each runway carries its approach cones. The red sea is past the offshore limit: 15 km off the coast or 1 km inside the world's edge, whichever comes first; the orange band before it is where the warning shows. What changes

Civil airports: the one by the western cities, where it was, and a second in the north, on the flattest ground at least 8 km from the first (its 59 m of rise levelled). Seaports: the west coast's, where it was, and a second on the north coast. Bases: 0, 1, 2 and 3 stay; base 4 goes, and base 3 moves 1 km to clear the new sites. Every site has a road from its city.

The offshore limit

The coast lies 9 to 15 km from the island's middle and the world ends 20.5 km out, so 15 km of sea fits only toward the corners: on most headings the world's edge, less 1 km, is what stops you. Past the line the mission fails, free roam too, and you come back seated in your mech in the nearest city.
