What changes

Civil airports: the one we have, by the western cities, and a second in the eastern desert, placed before anything military so they get the flat ground. Seaports: the one on the west coast and a second on the east coast, a ship lane round the south between them. Bases: 0, 1, 2 and 3 stay (west, east, south, north); 4 goes (crowded between 1 and 3). Base 2 stands 3.4 km from airport 1, its runway parallel and 2.85 km south of the airport's, so their approach cones do not cross.

The offshore limit

The coast lies 9 to 15 km from the island's middle and the world ends 20.5 km out, so 15 km of sea fits only toward the corners: on most headings the world's edge, less 1 km, is what stops you. Past the line the mission fails, free roam too, and you come back seated in your mech in the nearest city.
