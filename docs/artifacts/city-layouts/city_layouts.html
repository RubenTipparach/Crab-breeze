Today

The real rules of cb_core::world::layout: one flat square, a straight grid of 70-110 m blocks with a 36 m arterial every fourth street, every block cut into equal 18-36 m lots, one box on each, taller toward the middle (up to 60 storeys), 6 % of blocks parks, 8 % of lots plazas, a ring of house rows round the edge, straight highways out.

Future

After Fargo, the owner's reference: a highway across the city with almost all the commerce along it (big-box stores, car lots, gas stations and their car parks), a small downtown with a few towers beside it, and block after block of houses with yards, alleys, garages and trees, on grids that drift a few degrees between neighbourhoods; parks, schools and corner shops among them, an industrial park, a ragged edge into farmland. Try the variations: a small or large downtown (a large one takes the towers and grows the housing round it, with apartment blocks near the centre), compact, along one main street or in two cores; the highway at grade (only arterials cross it), under overpasses (every street bridges over it on embankments), elevated on a viaduct (the streets run on under it), or a bypass round the edge with a spur avenue into town; inland or on a coast. It meets the town at real exits: a SPUI by the downtown, diamonds, a single quadrant, a partial cloverleaf and a cloverleaf at the ends, a trumpet on the bypass. No rivers (they were taken out of the game).
