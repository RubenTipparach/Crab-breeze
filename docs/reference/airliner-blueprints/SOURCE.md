# Airliner blueprints (reference for modelling)

Three-view line drawings from NASA Dryden (now Armstrong) Flight Research Center's graphics
collection, as re-hosted on nasa.gov in 2023 (`https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/eg-NNNN-NN.png`).
They are United States government works: public domain in the United States, not literally CC0. NASA's
own note says the drawings are not engineering-accurate, so sizes are taken from the published
dimensions below and the drawings give the shape only. Downloaded 2026-10-29, unchanged.

| File | NASA id | Aircraft | Real size |
|---|---|---|---|
| `douglas-c47-dc3.png` | EG-0016-01 | Douglas C-47 / DC-3 | 19.7 m long, 29.0 m span |
| `beechcraft-king-air-b200.png` | EG-0057-01 | Beechcraft King Air B200 | 13.3 m long, 16.6 m span |
| `boeing-720.png` | EG-0009-01 | Boeing 720 | 41.3 m long, 39.9 m span |
| `four-jet-narrowbody-eg0023.png` | EG-0023-01 | four-jet narrowbody, likely Convair 990 (unconfirmed) | about 42 m long |
| `lockheed-l1011-tristar.png` | EG-0058-01 | Lockheed L-1011 TriStar | 54.2 m long, 47.3 m span |
| `mcdonnell-douglas-md11.png` | EG-0064-01 | McDonnell Douglas MD-11 | 61.6 m long, 51.7 m span |
| `boeing-747-sca.png`, `boeing-747-sca-side.png` | EG-0012-01, -02 | Boeing 747 Shuttle Carrier | 70.6 m long, 59.6 m span |

`preview-sheet.jpg` puts them side by side (lines darkened for reading).

The game models are Crab Breeze's own 1980s airliners built from these proportions, with no
real airline or maker livery or name.
