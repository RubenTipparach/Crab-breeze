# Sound effect sources

Every file here is synthesised by `tools/audio/gen_sfx.py` (rebuild: `tools/audio/render_sfx.sh`),
from oscillators, noise, filters and envelopes, with no recorded material: they belong to the
project outright. They are placeholders voiced electronic and futuristic (owner, 2026-10-25). A
recorded sound that replaces one keeps the cue name and gets a row below with its URL, author,
licence (survey P4: CC0, CC-BY with a credits screen) and every edit made to it. Candidates:
`docs/analysis/audio-sources.md`.
