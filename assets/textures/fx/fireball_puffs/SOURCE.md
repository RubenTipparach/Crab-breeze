# fireball_puffs

Rendered from `tools/materials/ptex/fireball_puffs.ptex` by Material Maker 174f15e, headless on lavapipe (`material-maker-headless` skill), then `tools/materials/postprocess.py`: maps albedo at 1024 px. Use: a fireball's billows, the smoke puffs' shapes burning, cross-faded into smoke as they cool.
