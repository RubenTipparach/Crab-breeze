# STRATOSTACEAN: How to Play

## Getting Started

- Walk to your mech and press **E** at the ladder
- The mech kneels, opens, and you climb into the seat
- Everything is first person, from the cockpit

## Tips

- Skates and jets are limited: watch heat on the MFDs
- Hold **Ctrl** to look around the cabin and click the screens
- Read your mail for jobs: press **M**, or **Esc** then **Mail**
- Accept a job to get a mission; enemies only arrive for an accepted one
- Buildings fall: use them, or bring them down

## On Foot

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| W A S D | Walk |
| Mouse | Look |
| Shift | Run |
| E | Board the mech |

## Piloting

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| W / S | Throttle |
| A / D | Turn |
| Mouse | Aim torso |
| X | Throttle to zero |
| 1 / 2 / 3 | Walk / Skate / Jet |
| Shift | Toggle skates |
| Space (hold) | Rocket lift |
| C | Dive / sink |
| Ctrl (hold) | Free look, click screens |
| E | Climb out |

## Combat

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Left mouse | Fire selected weapon |
| Right mouse | Cannon |
| Mouse wheel / ] | Change weapon |
| Q | Next target |
| F | Missile at lock |
| Z / V | Left / right punch |
| Tab | Close combat on / off |

## Other

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| M | Read mail (jobs) |
| Esc | Menu (Mail, Options, Controls) |
